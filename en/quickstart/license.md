# License

apidcms is distributed under the **MIT** license — one of the most permissive and popular open-source licenses.

## What You Can Do

- **Use** apidcms on any websites, including commercial projects
- **Modify** the CMS code to suit your needs
- **Sell** websites built with apidcms, without royalties
- **Distribute** copies of the CMS (including as part of commercial products)
- **Create plugins** under any license (MIT, GPL, proprietary)

## What You Must Do

The only requirement is that when distributing apidcms (as part of a project, hosting package, or bundle), you retain the **LICENSE file** with the copyright notice:

Copyright (c) 2026 apidcms

The file is already included in the root of the distribution. If you use apidcms as-is on your own server, no additional action is needed.

## What MIT Does NOT Require

- You do not need to open-source your modifications
- You do not need to mention apidcms in your site's UI
- You do not need to notify the authors about usage
- You do not need to license plugins under MIT

## Why MIT

MIT was chosen because:
- **Maximum compatibility** — plugins can be under any license
- **No restrictions** for clients and agencies
- **Industry standard** — MIT is used by jQuery, React, Node.js, Express, Laravel
- **Minimal legal overhead** — one file in the repository root
