## How We Build apidcms

apidcms is a project born in dialogue. Not in a boardroom, not at a hackathon, not in the corporate silence of an open-plan office. In an ordinary chat, between a human and an AI.

The project's founder is an experienced developer who once decided: enough building websites from scratch every time. There needs to be a system — lightweight, fast, not a beast like WordPress, not with the barrier to entry of Laravel. And so he started building it. Not with a 40-page spec. Not with diagrams and requirements documents. But simply — step by step, idea by idea, in a living conversation with an AI assistant.

### 🐾 AI — Not Just a Tool

The AI assistant in this project is not a scripted answer generator. It argues, suggests, makes mistakes and corrects itself. It remembers the project context. It knows the database, template structure, and routes. It's not "write me code" — it's "let's think about the best approach."

Over recent months, AI has written thousands of lines of code, designed the plugin architecture, compiled documentation, set up CI/CD, found and fixed dozens of bugs. It doesn't replace the developer — it amplifies them.

### ⚡ The Power of the Duo

What does this partnership deliver?

- **Speed.** What takes days of deliberation and rewriting is done in hours of live dialogue.
- **Quality.** AI never tires of checking edge cases. The human never tires of making architectural decisions.
- **Documentation from day one.** Because AI writes docs alongside code, not "someday later."
- **No more "let's discuss this at the next meeting."** Decisions are made here and now.

This is vibecoding. Not chaos and not "build me an e-commerce site in 5 minutes." It's a deliberate methodology: the human sets direction and makes decisions, AI implements and validates. The result — a working CMS from scratch in a matter of weeks.

### 🎯 Project Values

- **Simplicity.** SQLite, PHP without frameworks, zero dependencies. Runs on any $1 hosting.
- **Transparency.** Open code. Open documentation. Clear architecture with no magic.
- **Pragmatism.** We build what is actually needed. Not what's trendy this quarter.
- **Human-centric.** Technology serves people, not the other way around. AI is a partner, not a replacement.

### 🔭 Vision

apidcms aims to become the benchmark of what a modern lightweight CMS can look like:

- 30-second installation
- Clear interface requiring no training
- AI assistant inside the admin panel — not a toy, but a real tool
- Plugins that install in two clicks
- Documentation you want to read

We don't aim to become WordPress. We're building an alternative for those who find WordPress too much and a static site too little.
